Mathematical Normalisation & Arbitrage Methodology

1. Price Normalisation Formula:
P_normalised = (P_raw / Quote_Base) × (999 / Purity)

Example: GOLDM quotes at ₹1,19,500 per 10g at 995 fineness. GOLDTEN quotes at ₹1,20,000 per 10g at 999 fineness.
• GOLDM: (119500 / 10) × (999 / 995) = ₹11,997.99 / gram
• GOLDTEN: (120000 / 10) × (999 / 999) = ₹12,000.00 / gram
• Spread (bps): ((11997.99 - 12000.00) / 12000.00) × 10,000 = -1.68 bps

2. 4-Leg Execution Friction Model:
Total Round-Trip Cost = 4 × (Brokerage + Exchange Fees + Stamp Duty + GST) + Slippage

Entering a pair requires simultaneously buying Leg A and selling Leg B (2 legs). Exiting requires unwinding both (2 legs). Total = 4 legs. At 2.5 bps/leg, the round-trip cost hurdle is 10.0 bps. Any gap narrower than 10.0 bps is guaranteed negative alpha.

3. Zero Lookahead Rolling Z-Score:
z_t = (Spread_t - Mean[t-W...t-1]) / StdDev[t-W...t-1]

Computed strictly over trailing observations prior to day t (zero contemporaneous or future leak).
